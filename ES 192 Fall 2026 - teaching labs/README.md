# ES 192 Fall 2026 – teaching labs

Interactive, browser-based labs for ES 192 (Materials Selection in Mechanical Design), Harvard SEAS, Fall 2026.
Each lab is a single offline HTML file: download it and open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. No install and no internet needed.

| Lab | Week | What students do |
|---|---|---|
| [Shape Efficiency Lab](Shape%20Efficiency%20Lab/) | 8 | Load five 3D test rigs (columns, thin-walled tubes, cellular beams, printed lattices, corrugated sheet) and watch the grains and atoms yield, slip and break |
| [Crystal Lab](https://seymurhh.github.io/es192-crystal-lab/) | 9–10 | Bonds, unit cells, stacking, a cooling atom raft, dislocations and phase diagrams (separate repo: `Seymurhh/es192-crystal-lab`) |
